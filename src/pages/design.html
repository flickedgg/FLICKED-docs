The website is the reference implementation, and its tokens live in website/app/globals.css. This site uses the same tokens.

Principles

Clean and quiet

Dark, neutral, lots of space. The calm of a Valve product page, not a gaming RGB setup.

One accent

Orange is the only saturated colour. If everything is orange, nothing is.

The product is the picture

Show real UI (scoreboards, queues, server status) instead of decoration. No stock art, no abstract 3D.

Plain words, and honest

Anyone should understand a label without CS jargon. FLICKED is alpha: say so, and never fake numbers.

Tried and removed

These looked "cool" and made the product feel cheap. Do not bring them back: laser beams and glowing blobs behind content, custom crosshair cursors, text scramble effects, italic or skewed headings and buttons, animated counters, spotlight-on-hover cards, floating collage hero layouts, and em dashes in copy.

Components

Buttons

Primary is primary fill with near-black text, hover #ff6414. Outline is transparent with a .14 border, .28 on hover. 42px tall, 6px radius. One primary button per view.

Status, tags and progress

Live Starting Finished de_mirage

Status is always a dot plus a word, never colour alone. Progress bars are 4px tall on a .08 track.

Cards

surface background, 12px radius, .08 border. Inside: a 40px icon tile with an orange tint, an 18px title, one sentence, and a small labelled picture of the feature. Hover only brightens the border: no lifts, no glows.

Icons and motion

  • Icons are line icons on a 24px grid with a 1.7px stroke, round caps and joins, no fill. Lucide matches. 20px in a 40px tile; 16px in buttons.
  • Motion is quiet and never needed to understand the UI: 150 to 250ms for state changes, easing cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1), and prefers-reduced-motion always respected.

Writing

  • Plain language. "Damage per round", not "ADR". "Match replays", not "Demos".
  • When a CS term is the right word, keep it and explain it once nearby.
  • No em dashes. Use a full stop, comma or colon.
  • Sentence case for buttons and labels: "Get started", "Report an issue".
  • Be upfront about alpha status and missing features.

The launcher

The launcher renders web UI, so the tokens apply directly, at tool density: section padding drops to 24 to 32px and headings to the h3 size or smaller. A dark custom titlebar with a .06 hairline, a left sidebar with 36px nav items, and one orange action on screen ("Find match", "Accept", "Connect"). The match found dialog is the one place a bigger moment is fine: a plane-2 dialog with the countdown and ten player slots, filled orange when accepted.
